The Problem

Building maintenance is a mess.

Most buildings still run maintenance through group chats, sticky notes, and gut feeling. The result? Chaos.

💬
The WhatsApp chaos
Repair requests buried in group chats. Landlords miss messages. Tenants never know if anyone saw their request.
📵
No record keeping
When a problem reoccurs, nobody can prove it was reported before. No history, no accountability.
🕐
Zero transparency
Tenants submit a request and hear nothing for weeks. Is it being handled? Who knows.
📞
Constant phone tag
Scheduling a repair takes 10 calls. What time? Is someone home? Can we enter? All via text.
Features

Everything in one platform.

Built specifically for apartment buildings. Not adapted from enterprise software.

🎫
Ticketing System
Every repair request tracked from submission to completion. Nothing falls through the cracks.
🚨
Emergency Mode
Gas leak, flooding, no heat — tenants trigger an emergency alert straight to the landlord.
📅
Appointment Scheduling
Landlords offer time slots. Tenants pick one. No more back-and-forth about availability.
📷
Before & After Photos
Workers upload completion photos. No more "we already fixed it" disputes.
📊
Priority Scoring
Every ticket gets a 0–100 score based on urgency, safety risk, and time open.
💬
Direct Messages
Landlords and tenants can message each other privately, separate from ticket threads.
💰
Cost Tracker
Log labor and parts per repair. See monthly maintenance spending at a glance.
⭐
Tenant Ratings
After each repair, tenants rate the work. Landlords know who their best workers are.
🌐
Multilingual
English, Spanish, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, French — tenants see the app in their language.
How It Works

Three roles. One platform.

Every user sees only what's relevant to them.

🏠
Landlord / Manager
Role 1
See all requests sorted by priority. Assign workers. Schedule appointments. Track costs. Monitor your entire building in one dashboard.
🏡
Tenant
Role 2
Submit requests in seconds. Track status in real time. Get notified when a repair is scheduled. Rate the work when done.
🔨
Repair Worker
Role 3
See only your assigned jobs. Upload before/after photos. Submit quotes. Mark jobs complete. No more lost instructions.
